# Sales One-Pagers — n8n Automation Pack

## 1. Instant Lead Responder (for clinics, hotels, real-estate)
**Pain:** "Website se enquiry aati hai, par hum 2–4 ghante baad dekhte hain —
tab tak customer doosri clinic/hotel ko call kar chuka hota hai."
**Pitch:** "Aapke website form se enquiry aate hi — 5 second me — customer ko
personalised reply mil jata hai, aur har lead score ke saath aapki Google Sheet
me log ho jati hai: HOT (30 min me call karo), WARM (2 ghante), COLD (24 ghante).
Koi lead miss nahi, koi manual entry nahi."
**Demo line:** "Abhi isi call pe test karte hain — main sample enquiry bhejta
hoon, aap reply dekho."

## 2. Appointment Booker + Reminders (for clinics especially)
**Pain:** "Receptionist chhutti pe ho ya busy ho to appointment miss ho jati
hai, aur no-shows se chair-time waste hota hai."
**Pitch:** "Patient website se slot book kare → turant Google Calendar me entry
+ confirmation email → 24 ghante pehle automatic reminder. Receptionist ka
phone-uthana wala kaam khatam; no-shows girte hain."
**Demo line:** "Main abhi ek dummy booking karta hoon — aap calendar me entry
aur confirmation email dono dekho."

## 3. Pricing anchor (same as AI-agent slabs)
- Starter setup **₹25,000** one-time (one workflow, deployed + tested).
- Growth setup **₹45,000** one-time (workflows + sheet/CRM wiring + 1 month support).
- Care plan **₹4,999/month** optional.
**Objection handling:**
- "Humare paas pehle se receptionist hai" → "Ye usko replace nahi karta —
  uske chhutti/busy/after-hours ke gaps cover karta hai."
- "Data safe rahega?" → "Aapke apne Google account me chalta hai — Sheet,
  Calendar, Gmail sab aapka. Humare paas kuch store nahi hota."
- "Technical hai, hum nahi sambhalenge" → "Setup hum karke denge; roz ka
  chalana zero-maintenance hai. Kuch atke to care plan hai."
